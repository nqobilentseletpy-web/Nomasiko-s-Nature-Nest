Our Story

"Where every strand blooms."

The Founder

Nqobile Ntsele

Nomasiko's Nature Nest began with one woman, one chair and a deep love for the earth.

Named in honour of the women who raised her, Nqobile Ntsele built the Nest as a place where beauty and wellbeing grow side by side. Inspired by the gardens, herbs and healing traditions she grew up around, she set out to create a salon that feels less like an appointment and more like coming home.

What started as a single studio has blossomed into four havens in Sandton, Hartbeespoort, Ballito and Camps Bay, each one warm, welcoming and thoughtfully designed with natural textures, living plants and plant-based products.
